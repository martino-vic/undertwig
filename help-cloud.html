Google Cloud connection help

Use this checklist if Undertwig shows “Not connected to Google Cloud.”

What “connected” means

When you are signed in, Undertwig stores your project in your Google Drive application data folder (hidden app storage for Undertwig). That data counts against your Google account storage and is free within your Drive quota. It does not appear as a normal folder in the Drive file list.

Quick checks

  1. Confirm you are signed in to Undertwig with Google.
  2. Under the file tree, click Connect and finish the Google permission popup (you may have up to about two minutes — do not ignore a background tab/window).
  3. After Connect succeeds once, reloads should show green automatically (token is kept for the tab session).
  4. Use the same site origin you authorized (for example https://www.undertwig.com).

Brave browser

Brave often blocks Google’s permission window even when “popups” look allowed. Check both of these:

  1. Site popups: click the Brave lion / site controls on the left of the address bar → Site settings (or the padlock/tune icon) → Popups and redirects → Allow for www.undertwig.com.
  2. Shields: click the lion icon → set Shields to Down for undertwig.com, or leave Shields up but allow cookies / disable aggressive blocking for this site. Google sign-in needs access to accounts.google.com.
  3. Optional: Settings → Shields → Content filtering / cookie settings — allow third-party cookies for Google accounts if Connect still fails.
  4. Look for a blocked-popup icon in the address bar right after clicking Connect. If Brave blocked it, choose Always allow and click Connect again.

Google Cloud Console (project owners)

  1. Open the undertwig Google Cloud project.
  2. Enable the Google Drive API.
  3. Under OAuth Data Access / scopes, include https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata.
  4. Under Clients, ensure Authorized JavaScript origins include your exact site origin (https://www.undertwig.com and/or https://undertwig.com).
  5. If the OAuth app is in Testing, add your Google account under Test users.

Still stuck?

  • Sign out of Undertwig, sign back in, and accept Drive permission again.
  • In your Google Account permissions, remove Undertwig access and reconnect from the editor.
  • Check the browser console for origin_mismatch or GSI/Drive errors.
  • Open an issue on GitHub.
