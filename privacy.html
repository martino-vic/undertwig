Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Undertwig (“we”, “us”) is a browser-based LaTeX workspace available at undertwig.com. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the service.

Summary

Undertwig is designed to run in your browser. Your project files are stored on your device. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not intentionally collect the contents of your documents.

Information stored on your device

The editor saves your current project in your browser’s local storage (under a key such as undertwig-latex-project-v1) so your work can persist between visits on the same browser and device. That data stays on your device unless you export it, clear site data, or otherwise share it.

Generated PDFs are created locally in your browser and are not uploaded to Undertwig servers.

Information processed to provide the service

  • Hosting and delivery. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most websites, the hosting provider may process standard technical request data such as IP address, user agent, and request timestamps in server logs for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.
  • Optional package fetching. When a document needs TeX packages that are not already available in your project, the local compiler may request package resources from a third-party TeX Live endpoint (currently texlive.texlyre.org). Those requests are made by your browser and may include technical request metadata.
  • Source repository. If you visit our public GitHub repository or open issues there, GitHub’s own privacy terms apply to that activity.

Cookies and similar technologies

Undertwig does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. The app relies on browser local storage (and normal browser/session mechanisms) to keep your project available on your device.

How we use information

Technical hosting data, where processed by our hosting provider, is used only to operate, secure, and maintain the website. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your document contents for advertising.

Data retention

Project data in local storage remains until you clear it or your browser removes it. Hosting provider logs are retained according to that provider’s practices. We do not maintain a separate Undertwig account database.

Children

Undertwig is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

International visitors

Because the site is delivered over the internet, technical request data may be processed in the United States or other locations used by GitHub Pages and any package endpoint your browser contacts.

Your choices

  • Clear site data in your browser to remove locally stored projects.
  • Export or copy your files before clearing data if you want to keep them.
  • Use network controls in your browser if you prefer to block third-party package requests.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project’s GitHub repository: github.com/martino-vic/undertwig.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we publish revisions.
